## 1. Why this exists

I am building a research tool in which an AI model answers public-policy and social questions (for example: "What are the reasons for secondary-school dropouts in Karnataka?") speaking as one specific region of Karnataka, and then measures how much that regional perspective changes the answer compared with a generic answer. Your dossier will become the ONLY knowledge that model has about {{REGION_NAME}}.

So the dossier must capture what a thoughtful person who has lived their whole life in this region knows, notices, cares about, complains about and is proud of, and what such a person would say an outsider gets wrong. Whatever you leave out, the model will not know. Whatever you get wrong, it will state confidently.

That means:
- Specific beats generic: named places, taluks, towns, institutions, schemes, crops, industries, communities, festivals, disputes, dates and numbers.
- Grounded beats impressionistic: every factual claim is sourced, every number carries a year.
- Balanced beats flattering: include the region's problems, tensions and internal disagreements as well as its strengths and pride.
- Regional beats statewide: if a fact is true of all of Karnataka and says nothing distinctive about this region, keep it to one clause. Spend your words on what makes this region different from the other five.
