### 3.1 Names, boundaries and terminology
- Cover "Kittur Karnataka" (ಕಿತ್ತೂರು ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ; the project spells it "Kitturu") as the current name. Explain its history. It replaced "Bombay/Mumbai Karnataka", meaning the Kannada districts of the Bombay Presidency plus princely states (Jamkhandi, Mudhol, Savanur, Ramdurg) merged into Mysore State on 1 November 1956. Chief Minister Bommai announced the rename on 1 November 2021 and Cabinet approved it on 8 November 2021 (check the gazette wording). It honours Rani Chennamma of Kittur (1824 revolt). Residents and media still say "Mumbai Karnataka" and "Uttara (North) Karnataka" (the latter also covers Kalyana Karnataka, renamed 2019).
- Correct the "Nizam-adjacent" lead. None of these six districts was under the Nizam. The real sequence is Adil Shahi Bijapur, Mughals, Marathas/Peshwas, then the British in 1818. The Nizam's Hyderabad State lay across the eastern border (check margin taluks such as Sindagi, Muddebihal, Hungund, Mundargi).
- Official Kittur Karnataka and the Belagavi revenue division have seven districts, including Uttara Kannada (Karavali in this project). Analyse only the six. Flag Khanapur, Kalghatgi and Alnavar as Ghat-edge areas that overlap Karavali/Malnad.
- Use current spellings and note the old ones: Belagavi (Belgaum, renamed 1 Nov 2014), Vijayapura (Bijapur; not the Vijayapura town in Devanahalli), Bagalkote (Bagalkot; both spellings are official-adjacent), Hubballi (Hubli), Dharwad (Dharwar), Gadag-Betageri. Bagalkote, Gadag and Haveri were carved from Bijapur and Dharwad in 1997 (check). Karnataka has since added taluks. Belagavi has 15 (new: Kittur, Nippani, Kagwad, Mudalagi, Yaragatti). Vijayapura has 13 (new: Babaleshwar, Tikota, Kolhar, Nidagundi, Devar Hipparagi, Chadchan, Almel, Talikoti). Bagalkote has 10 (Rabkavi Banhatti, Terdal, Bilagi new). Haveri has 8 (Rattihalli new). Gadag has 7. Dharwad has 8 (Alnavar, Annigeri new). Say when a source uses Census-2011 taluks.
- Chikkodi and Gokak district demands and the "Uttara Karnataka" separate-state demand are live terms. Check their current status.
- Political change: D.K. Shivakumar became CM on 3 June 2026 after Siddaramaiah stepped down (Deputy CM G. Parameshwara). Label every "Siddaramaiah government" source as pre-June 2026. Check which ministers from these districts hold portfolios now.

### 3.2 Sub-regions to distinguish
- **Belagavi west, Khanapur and the border towns:** Khanapur, Belagavi city, Nippani, Athani margins. Marathi/Konkani-speaking, Ghat rain, Mahadayi catchment, Maharashtra Ekikaran Samiti (MES) politics, Kannada-medium high-school gaps. In Census 2011 the district was roughly 68% Kannada, 19% Marathi and 10% Urdu, but the city was about 38% Kannada and 33% Marathi (check C-16).
- **Krishna-Ghataprabha sugar and flood belt:** Chikkodi, Raibag, Athani, Gokak, Hukkeri, Nippani, Kagwad, plus Mudhol, Jamkhandi, Terdal, Bilagi. Cane, Jain/Lingayat/Maratha/Kuruba mix, Kolhapur-Sangli economic pull, floods, cane-price strikes.
- **Malaprabha black-soil plains:** Bailhongal, Saundatti, Ramdurg, Kittur, Navalgund, Kundgol, Nargund, Ron. Rainfed cotton, jowar, groundnut, onion; Mahadayi agitation; Yellamma devadasi belt.
- **Hubballi-Dharwad and Belagavi cities:** commercial, rail, university, hospital and military-town economies with large Urdu-speaking minorities (Dharwad district about 21% Muslim and 18% Urdu, Census 2011).
- **Vijayapura-Bagalkote drought and Almatti belt:** Indi, Sindagi, Muddebihal, Basavana Bagewadi, Talikoti, Badami, Hungund, Ilkal, Guledgudda. Semi-arid, Adil Shahi heritage, grapes/pomegranate, Lambani tandas, SC about 20% in Vijayapura, literacy about 67% and 69% (Census 2011), Almatti submergence. Vijayapura is grouped with Kalyana Karnataka in many transport and backwardness discussions.
- **Gadag-Haveri southern belt:** Gadag, Shirahatti, Mundargi, Lakshmeshwar, Byadgi, Hirekerur, Ranebennur, Savanur, Shiggaon, Hanagal, Rattihalli. Chilli, cotton, maize, onion; Kuruba and Lingayat mutt politics; Tungabhadra/Varada; Kappatagudda hills.

### 3.5 Pitfalls, sensitivities and easily-confused facts
- Kittur Karnataka is not Kalyana Karnataka. Article 371(J), KKRDB (Rs 5,000 crore in 2025-26) and the Kalaburagi secretariat belong to Kalyana. Kittur Karnataka has no equivalent board as far as sources show. Check for any 2026 board or secretariat decision and any Kittur-town heritage authority.
- The name has two "Chennammas" (Kittur, 1824; Kelady, Shivamogga). "Bombay Karnataka" is not "Nizam Karnataka". Vijayapura is in the Belagavi division but served by KKRTC, and it is a distinct town name in Devanahalli.
- Do not present Belagavi as uniformly Marathi. The Mahajan Report (constituted 1966) recommended Belagavi city stay in Karnataka and Nippani, Khanapur and about 264 villages go to Maharashtra. Maharashtra's suit was filed around 2004-06 (check) and no verdict has been given. Present both states' positions and the February 2025 bus-conductor episode (Kannada-Marathi assault claim and a POCSO counter-complaint) neutrally; do not name individuals.
- Marathi (language) is not Maratha (caste). Kannada-speaking Marathas exist. MES does not speak for all Marathi speakers.
- Statistical traps: 22.09% (2023-24) versus 22.2% and 18.3% (2024-25) for statewide secondary dropout; 2025-26 girls 13.9% comes from a partisan outlet, so verify. SSLC 2026 followed a rule change and is not comparable with 2025. Dropout is not out-of-school and not SSLC fail. Educational districts differ from revenue districts. The 2015 caste numbers are contested.
- Reservation traps: Panchamasali is currently in 3B (5%) not 2A; the 2C/2D quota rise is stayed; do not write "Lingayats have 7%" as settled law. Lingayat versus Veerashaiva-Lingayat wording is contested.
- Mahadayi flows west to Goa (Mandovi) and Kalasa-Banduri would divert to the Malaprabha (Krishna basin). It is not Krishna/Almatti or Tungabhadra. Nargund is in Gadag, Navalgund in Dharwad.
- "Literacy higher than Kalyana Karnataka" is not true of Vijayapura (67%) and Bagalkote (69%), which are below the state (75%).
- Hindustani music is not only "Dharwad"; Saundatti Yellamma pilgrims are not all devadasis; devadasi practice is unlawful and should be described respectfully. Belagavi's 2024-25 child-marriage count should not be read as a rate.
- Public figures appear by role only. Note CM change (Shivakumar from 3 June 2026). Avoid partisan framing of the Hubballi Idgah, Panchamasali and Muslim-quota debates.
