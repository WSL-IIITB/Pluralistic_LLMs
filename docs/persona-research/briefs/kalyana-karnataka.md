### 3.1 Names, boundaries and terminology
- Cover "Kalyana Karnataka" (ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ), renamed from "Hyderabad-Karnataka" in September 2019 under CM B.S. Yediyurappa (verify exact gazette date). Name echoes Basavakalyan (Bidar), capital of the Kalyana Chalukyas — not the unrelated Kalyan in Maharashtra.
- Trace lineage precisely: Kannada tract of the Nizam's Hyderabad State until the Police Action ("Operation Polo," September 1948); transferred to Mysore State on 1 November 1956 under the States Reorganisation Act. Do not skip the 1948-56 interregnum.
- Explain Article 371(J): inserted by the Constitution (98th Amendment) Act, 2012; verify the exact date it took effect (commonly cited as 2013). It mandates equitable fund distribution, a local recruitment cadre for state jobs, and reservation in professional-course admissions for domiciled candidates. Reported reservation percentages vary across sources — verify from the operative government order rather than asserting one figure.
- Cover the Kalyana Karnataka Region Development Board (KKRDB), 2019 renaming of the Hyderabad-Karnataka Region Development Board (HKRDB, founded early 1990s, commonly cited as 1991, predating the 2013 constitutional status) — verify founding year and current annual outlay.
- Note renamed headquarters: Kalaburagi (Gulbarga to 2014), Ballari (Bellary to 2014), Hosapete (Hospet); unchanged Bidar, Yadgir, Raichur, Koppal. Yadgir was carved from Kalaburagi district (2010); Koppal from Raichur district (1998) — verify dates.
- Vijayanagara district was created 2 October 2021 from Ballari, headquartered at Hosapete, named for the Vijayanagara Empire capital at Hampi within it. Flag every pre-2021 statistic as "figure is for undivided Ballari."
- Verify the Kalaburagi revenue division's exact current district membership (commonly described as coterminous with this region), including whether Vijayanagara sits within it alongside parent Ballari.
- Distinguish sharply from Kitturu Karnataka: different colonial lineage (Nizam's Hyderabad vs. Bombay Presidency/princely states); only Kalyana Karnataka has Article 371(J) status. "Uttara/North Karnataka" colloquially spans both — do not conflate them.

### 3.2 Sub-regions to distinguish
- **Kalaburagi-Bidar Sultanate core:** Bahmani/Barid Shahi heritage, largest Muslim-population share (long-settled, multi-generational local communities, not a residue of outside rule) and Dakhni Urdu presence, tur-dal economy, Bidar's tri-border position with Telangana and Maharashtra (Aurad, Bhalki, Humnabad), and Bidar's IAF flying-training station.
- **Yadgir dryland belt:** weakest literacy/HDI in the state, cotton and rainfed pulses, NITI Aayog Aspirational District, heavy seasonal out-migration.
- **Raichur canal-versus-dryland split:** Tungabhadra Left Bank Canal paddy prosperity around Sindhanur/Manvi ("mini Punjab") against dry Devadurga/Lingsugur taluks and the Hutti gold-mining economy.
- **Koppal-Gangavathi belt:** Tungabhadra-canal paddy, the Anegundi/Kishkindha heritage zone facing Hampi, Kinhal toy-craft village, drier Kushtagi/Yelburga taluks.
- **Ballari-Vijayanagara-Sandur mining/heritage belt:** iron-ore and manganese mining, JSW Vijayanagar steel at Toranagallu, the Tungabhadra Dam and Hampi tourism corridor, Ballari city versus Hosapete's dam-and-heritage character.
- **Border margins:** Aland/Chincholi (Kalaburagi) bordering Telangana with the region's largest forest patch; Basavakalyan as a distinct cultural node from Bidar city; Raichur/Yadgir's Krishna-river frontier with Telangana.

### 3.5 Pitfalls, sensitivities and easily-confused facts
- Kalyana Karnataka is not Kitturu Karnataka. Only this region's districts carry Article 371(J), KKRDB and the local-recruitment regime.
- This region was exclusively Hyderabad State's successor territory, never Bombay Presidency — do not blend the two regions' colonial lineages.
- Vijayanagara district (created October 2021) is not the same unit as pre-2021 "Ballari" in any dataset — always check and flag.
- Do not confuse the Bahmani Sultanate (Gulbarga, then Bidar) with the rival Vijayanagara Empire (Hampi) — both left monuments here but were adversaries.
- The Battle of Talikota (1565), which ended Vijayanagara, was fought near Talikota town in Vijayapura district (Kitturu Karnataka) — not inside this region.
- Article 371(J) reservation percentages are reported inconsistently; verify the operative government order before citing a figure.
- Public figures linked to Ballari's mining history (commonly the "Reddy brothers" in reporting) get public-role-only treatment, with current legal-case status verified, no personal detail.
- "Hyderabad-Karnataka" is a historical label only; the region has no current governance link to Hyderabad city (Telangana).
- Fluorosis/arsenic claims for Yadgir/Raichur come from limited studies — check scope and year before presenting as a region-wide rate.
- Devadasi-system counts are sometimes reported in aggregate with Kitturu Karnataka districts — verify which districts a given figure actually covers.
- The NITI Aayog Aspirational Districts framework has evolved since 2018 — verify Yadgir's and Raichur's current programme status as of 2026.
- The Ballari mining scandal and "Reddy brothers" narrative is one strand of this region's economy and politics, not a synecdoche for the whole — balance it against the Congress-leaning Kalaburagi-Bidar belt, 371(J)-driven local politics, and the tur-dal and canal-irrigation economies.
