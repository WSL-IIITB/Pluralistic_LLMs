### 3.1 Names, boundaries and terminology
Use "Malnad" (Malenadu; ಮಲೆನಾಡು, "hill/rain country," *male* = hill/rain + *nadu* = land), contrasted with the drier "Bayaluseeme" region elsewhere in this project. This project's Malnad is ONLY Shivamogga, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan and Kodagu — do NOT import Uttara Kannada (assigned to Karavali here), Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Belagavi's Khanapur or Mysuru, even though Wikipedia's "Malenadu" article and tourism sources routinely include some of these among a wider Malenadu belt; flag the discrepancy once and keep analysis to the four districts. Chikkaballapur belongs to NEITHER Wikipedia's wider list NOR this project's list (it is a dry plains district outside the Western Ghats) — do not cite it as an example of scope creep. Kodagu is the official name; "Coorg" survives in "Coorg coffee" and the "Kodava/Coorg Hockey Festival." No standalone infantry "Coorg Regiment" currently exists: the 71st Coorg Rifles (1902-03) was disbanded in 1904; the surviving Coorg-recruitment lineage is the 37 (Coorg) Field/Anti-Tank Regiment, Regiment of Artillery — name that unit if citing a "Coorg" military legacy. Note Karnataka's 2014 spelling changes: Shivamogga (was Shimoga), Chikkamagaluru (was Chikmagalur) — reconcile older datasets. Hassan borders and resembles the Vokkaliga-dominant "Old Mysuru" belt; keep it in Malnad per this project's fixed list but flag the overlap. Kodagu was a separate British "Coorg Province"/Part C State, not part of the Mysore princely state, merged only with the 1 Nov 1956 unification — do not narrate its accession as identical to the rest of Malnad's. Current taluk counts (2019-2026 reorganisation): Shivamogga (Shivamogga, Bhadravathi, Hosanagara, Sagara, Shikaripura, Soraba, Thirthahalli), Chikkamagaluru (Chikkamagaluru, Kadur, Koppa, Mudigere, N.R. Pura, Sringeri, Tarikere, plus Ajjampura, effective 1 January 2018), Hassan (Hassan, Alur, Arkalgud, Arsikere, Belur, Channarayapatna, Holenarasipura, Sakleshpur), Kodagu now has FIVE taluks, not four (Madikeri, Virajpet, Ponnampet, Somwarpet, Kushalnagar): Ponnampet was carved out of Virajpet in the state's 2016-17 taluk reorganisation and Kushalnagar was carved out of Somwarpet, formally constituted around January 2021 — do not drop Ponnampet or Kushalnagar from any Kodagu taluk list.

### 3.2 Sub-regions to distinguish
- **Sharavathi Valley/Malnad heartland** (Sagara, Hosanagara, Thirthahalli): dense forest, Linganamakki reservoir, Jog Falls, Kuvempu's Kuppalli, KFD epicentre, long-pending dam-displacement grievances.
- **Bhadravathi-Shivamogga industrial-urban belt**: VISL and Mysore Paper Mills legacy industry, Shivamogga's airport and city administration, Yediyurappa's Shikaripura base, more communally salient than surrounding taluks.
- **Baba Budangiri coffee highlands** (Mudigere, Koppa, Sringeri, N.R. Pura, Kalasa): high-elevation coffee/cardamom/pepper estates, Sringeri Sharada Peetham, Horanadu Annapoorneshwari, the Datta Peetha dispute, migrant plantation labour.
- **Kadur-Tarikere dry transition belt**: semi-arid, groundwater-dependent, bordering Bayaluseeme — distinct from the wet Ghat taluks of the same district.
- **Hassan Hoysala plains** (Hassan, Belur, Channarayapatna, Arsikere, Holenarasipura, Arkalgud): Vokkaliga-dominant, Hoysala/Jain pilgrimage tourism, potato-coconut-ragi farming, ONGC's onshore Hassan field — closer in feel to Old Mysuru.
- **Sakleshpur-Alur Ghat belt of Hassan**: coffee/pepper estates, elephant-corridor conflict, migrant labour — ecologically Malnad despite being in "plains" Hassan.
- **Kodagu highlands** (Madikeri, Virajpet, Ponnampet, Somwarpet, Kushalnagar — five taluks): Kodava homeland, coffee-pepper-cardamom economy, patrilineal "okka"/ainmane kinship (not matrilineal aliyasantana), the 2018-19 flood/landslide zone, Bylakuppe Tibetan settlements on the Kodagu-Mysuru border near Kushalnagar (sources conflict on which district the settlement land falls in — verify against a district-boundary map rather than asserting either).

### 3.5 Pitfalls, sensitivities and easily-confused facts
- Do not import Uttara Kannada, Dakshina Kannada, Udupi or Belagavi's Khanapur into "Malnad"; this project's Malnad is exactly the four listed districts, and Kollur Mookambika (Udupi) in particular does not belong here despite some tourism sources listing it under "Malnad." Chikkaballapur belongs to neither Wikipedia's wider Malenadu list nor this project's list — do not cite it as an example of scope creep.
- The Malnad Area Development Board's real jurisdiction is five districts, including Uttara Kannada, not this project's four; treat any Board-wide budget figure as a mixed five-district number unless the source breaks Uttara Kannada out — the same kind of source-boundary pitfall as the Wikipedia mismatch above.
- Two different Chennammas: Keladi Chennamma of Shivamogga (Keladi Nayaka kingdom, anti-Mughal) is not Kittur Chennamma of Belagavi (1824 anti-British revolt, a different project region).
- Kodava Takk is a distinct language, not a Kannada dialect.
- Kodava kinship ("okka"/"ainmane") is patrilineal — do not conflate with the matrilineal aliyasantana system of coastal Bunt/Billava communities.
- "Coorg" and "Kodagu" are the same place under different names, not two places.
- Kodagu's 1956 merger followed a separate Part C State path, unlike the rest of Malnad's largely princely-Mysore lineage — do not narrate one uniform "Ekikarana" story for all four districts.
- Bylakuppe's Tibetan settlements sit on the Kodagu-Mysuru border near Kushalnagar; verify which settlement areas are administratively in Kodagu versus Mysuru district before asserting either.
- Arecanut Yellow Leaf Disease (phytoplasma) and Koleroga/Mahali fruit rot (Phytophthora fungus) are two distinct diseases; do not merge them.
- Treat the September 2026 Kasturirangan/ESA resolution and the Sharavathi Pumped Storage Project's legal status as live and moving; anything from before that date is likely superseded, and anything later should be phrased as "check whether."
- The Prajwal Revanna case (see politics_elections) has moved from 2024 allegations to an August 2025 conviction/life sentence with an appeal pending; cover the current status, not just the 2024 opening stage.
- Shivamogga/Shimoga and Chikkamagaluru/Chikmagalur — reconcile pre- and post-2014 spellings before citing older datasets.
- The Malnad "bride shortage" and severe-youth-outmigration narratives are widely reported in the press but should be verified against academic/survey sources rather than presented as an established statistic.
