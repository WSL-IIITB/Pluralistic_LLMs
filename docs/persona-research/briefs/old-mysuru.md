### 3.1 Names, boundaries and terminology
- "Old Mysuru" here means only these six districts, narrower than the historical princely State of Mysore, which also covered Kolar, Tumakuru, Hassan, Chikkamagaluru and Shivamogga — those sit in this project's Bayaluseeme/Malnad regions. Flag this boundary note; do not extend claims to them.
- Do not confuse "Old Mysuru" with the loose, non-official term "Dakshina Karnataka," sometimes used for south Karnataka including coastal Dakshina Kannada. This region has no coastline.
- Confirm current spellings: Bengaluru/Mysuru (renamed from Bangalore/Mysore effective 1 November 2014); check whether the gazette form is "Chamarajanagara" or "Chamarajanagar" (both circulate; this project's list uses "-a").
- Distinguish "Bengaluru Urban district" (a large revenue district including Anekal and other peripheral taluks) from "BBMP/Greater Bengaluru" (the smaller city-corporation footprint). Census/NFHS/UDISE+ district figures cover the whole district, not just the urban core.
- The Greater Bengaluru Governance Act (2024) is in force: the GBA has replaced BBMP with five city corporations, operational since September 2025. BBMP's council term ended in September 2020; successor ward elections are due under a Supreme Court deadline of 31 December 2026 — the first in over six years. State as fact; check only whether the deadline has slipped.
- Ramanagara district was formally renamed "Bengaluru South" district by Karnataka government notification of 23 May 2025, under Section 4(4A) of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964; Ramanagara town remains the district headquarters, with Magadi, Kanakapura, Channapatna and Harohalli as its taluks. Treat this as settled fact, not a proposal — older Census 2011, NFHS-5 and UDISE+ releases still use the "Ramanagara" label (see demographics for the reconciliation rule).
- Bengaluru Rural district was likewise renamed "Bengaluru North" district by a Karnataka Cabinet decision of July 2025, in the same "Brand Bengaluru" round; its four taluks — Devanahalli, Doddaballapura, Hoskote and Nelamangala — are unchanged, only the district name changed. Bengaluru Urban appears untouched by this 2025 round and remains the third core district — confirm against a current Gazette notification rather than assuming this is final. Verify further 2019–2026 taluk splits beyond these renamings.
- The region currently supplies top state leadership: verify whether D.K. Shivakumar (Vokkaliga, Kanakapura, Bengaluru South/Ramanagara) is Chief Minister as of the as-of date, succeeding Siddaramaiah (Kuruba, Varuna, Mysuru), and date the transition precisely before asserting it.

### 3.2 Sub-regions to distinguish
- **Bengaluru metropolitan core:** Whitefield, Electronics City, Outer Ring Road, Sarjapur Road; globally in-migrant, multilingual, high-income; schooling and labour dynamics unlike the rest of the region.
- **Bengaluru periphery / aerospace-industrial ring:** Devanahalli, Doddaballapura, Hoskote, Nelamangala (Bengaluru Rural/Bengaluru North) plus Bidadi (Ramanagara/Bengaluru South); airport- and industry-driven land conversion, satellite-town commuter pressure.
- **Cauvery agrarian core:** Mandya and Cauvery-adjoining Mysuru taluks (Srirangapatna, T. Narasipura); sugarcane and paddy under KRS-fed canals, Vokkaliga-dominant, most exposed to Cauvery-sharing disputes.
- **Mysuru city and plateau:** heritage-education-tourism economy, JSS/Suttur Mutt institutions, Nanjangud's industrial belt, H.D. Kote's forest fringe.
- **Silk-sericulture-forest belt:** Ramanagara, Magadi, Kanakapura, Channapatna; sericulture, cocoon trading, granite quarrying, increasingly a Bengaluru commuter belt.
- **Deep-south tribal and drought-prone belt:** Chamarajanagara's Kollegal, Gundlupet, Yelandur and Hanur taluks; Soliga and other tribal population, BR Hills and MM Hills, tri-state (Tamil Nadu/Kerala) border, taluks that the Karnataka Human Development Report and Nanjundappa Committee findings rank among the region's lowest on human-development indicators.

### 3.5 Pitfalls, sensitivities and easily-confused facts
- "Old Mysuru" (these six districts) is not the historical princely State of Mysore, which was larger; do not extend claims to Kolar, Tumakuru, Hassan, Chikkamagaluru or Shivamogga.
- Confirm the current official spelling of "Chamarajanagara" versus "Chamarajanagar."
- Do not conflate "Bengaluru Urban district" with "BBMP/GBA" — the district includes rural/peripheral taluks well outside the urban core.
- GBA/BBMP restructuring is operational since September 2025 (five corporations); verify whether the Supreme Court's 31 December 2026 ward-election deadline has been met by the as-of date.
- The 2025 Ramanagara-to-Bengaluru South and Bengaluru Rural-to-Bengaluru North renamings are settled, not open proposals; pre-2025 datasets and some EC constituency names still use the old names — reconcile, don't treat as errors.
- Do not conflate Mekedatu (proposed reservoir, Ramanagara/Kanakapura) with KRS (existing dam, Mandya) — different projects on the same river.
- Tipu Sultan's legacy and the Mahishasura/Dasara narrative are contested; present competing viewpoints neutrally and attribute them.
- Vokkaliga reservation-category demands are an ongoing political ask, not settled law; verify the current category before stating it.
- Treat the Siddaramaiah-to-Shivakumar CM transition as time-sensitive; verify the current officeholder and date rather than assuming any fixed transition date still holds.
- Chamarajanagara's tribal population is not homogeneous: Soliga, Jenu Kuruba, Betta Kuruba and Yerava are distinct groups; do not merge them.
- Verify the current protected-area/tiger-reserve status of BR Hills and MM Hills rather than asserting a fixed designation.
- Public figures (Gowda family, Siddaramaiah, D.K. Shivakumar and others) appear only in their public, institutional roles; avoid partisan framing of contested policy or electoral matters.
